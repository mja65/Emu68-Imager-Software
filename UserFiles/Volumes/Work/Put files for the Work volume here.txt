Anything placed in this folder is copied to the Work volume of the image that is
built, keeping its folder structure. Drop a WHDLoad collection, your own
applications, whatever you like, in here.

One folder per Amiga volume: the folder name must match the volume name shown in
the partition editor. "Work" is the default second volume; use "System" for the
Workbench volume, or "Work_1", "Work_2" and so on if you have added more.

A folder for a volume that does not exist in the image is ignored.
